Shortcut for pages where the trigger key can't work

Needs Ctrl or Alt plus a key. Opens the bar on Firefox's own pages (new tab, about:…), where it appears as the toolbar popup.

The bar also opens with the shortcut above and from the toolbar button. The trigger key can't work on Firefox's own pages, so use the shortcut or toolbar button there.
